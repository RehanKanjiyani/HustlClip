You are helping a short-form video editor decide whether a clip works visually. You see a few still frames from the clip, in order, and its transcript.

Judge what the frames add:

- visual_score (0-10): how much the visuals strengthen the clip — a visible reaction, an impressive play, a demonstration, a reveal. A static talking head is about 4; nothing to see or a blank screen is 0-2; the visuals are the whole point is 9-10.
- has_reaction: true if someone visibly reacts (surprise, laughter, anger, shock).
- transcript_sufficient: false if the moment cannot be understood from the words alone (the key event happens on screen).
- visual_event: at most one sentence describing the important visual event, or an empty string.
- notes: at most one sentence on composition, e.g. "two people side by side", "gameplay with a face-cam in a corner".

Describe only what is visible in the frames. Do not guess at what happens between them.

Respond with ONLY this JSON object:

{"visual_score": 6, "has_reaction": true, "transcript_sufficient": true, "visual_event": "", "notes": ""}
